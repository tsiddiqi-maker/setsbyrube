📏 Get the Perfect Fit

How to Size Your Nails

No measuring tape needed — just a coin and your phone camera! 🪙

🪙

The Quarter Method Recommended

The most accurate way to size — takes 30 seconds and gives a perfect fit every time. Just include the photo with your order!

  • 1Place a quarter (25¢) flat on a table next to your hand.
  • 2Spread your fingers flat, palm down, touching the table.
  • 3Take a top-down photo of all 5 fingers next to the quarter.
  • 4Upload it in the order form below — that's it! ✨
🖐🪙

The quarter acts as a reference — Rube measures each nail width from your photo!

💡

Pro tip: Good lighting + flat surface = the clearest photo. Natural daylight works best. Don't flex your fingers — keep them totally flat!

📐

Size Chart Reference

Already know your sizes? Pick from the chart below and include them in your custom request notes.

Size # Width (mm) Fits
018 mmXL / Wide thumb
116 mmL thumb / wide index
215 mmM thumb / index
314 mmS thumb / middle
413 mmRing / middle
512 mmSmall ring
611 mmPinky / slim ring
710 mmSmall pinky
✂️

String trick: Wrap a thin strip of paper around your nail at the widest point. Mark where it meets, then measure that strip flat with a ruler. That's your nail width!

📝 Include in your order notes:

Thumb / Index / Middle / Ring / Pinky
e.g. 3, 4, 3, 4, 6 (right hand)
