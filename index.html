Refund Policy

Every SetsByRube set is handmade to order just for you. Because each set is completely custom, we are unable to offer refunds once production has started.

If there's a problem with your order:

If your nails arrive damaged, incorrect, or there was a mistake on our end, please reach out within 7 days of receiving your order. We'll make it right — whether that's a replacement or a solution that works for you.

What we can't refund:

We can't offer refunds for change of mind, incorrect sizing provided by the customer, or sets that have been used/applied. Please measure carefully and follow the sizing guide!

Shipping issues:

If your order is lost in the mail, contact us on Instagram and we'll investigate with the carrier and work out a solution.

Questions? Slide into our DMs on Instagram @setsbyrubes — we're always happy to help 💖

📏 Get the Perfect Fit

How to Size Your Nails

No measuring tape needed — just a coin and your phone camera! 🪙

🪙

The Quarter Method Recommended

The most accurate way to size — takes 30 seconds and gives a perfect fit every time.

  • 1Place a quarter (25¢) flat on a table next to your hand.
  • 2Spread your fingers flat, palm down, touching the table.
  • 3Take a top-down photo of all 5 fingers next to the quarter.
  • 4Upload it in the order form below — that's it! ✨
🖐🪙

The quarter acts as a reference — Rube measures each nail width from your photo!

💡

Pro tip: Good lighting + flat surface = the clearest photo. Natural daylight works best.

📐

Size Chart Reference

Already know your sizes? Pick from the chart and include them in your order notes.
