📏 Fit Guide

Find Your Perfect Fit

Everything you need to know about measuring your nails, picking a size, choosing a shape, and deciding on length.

Step 1

How to Measure Your Nail Width

You'll need a soft measuring tape or a thin strip of paper and a ruler. Measure the widest part of each nail bed.

📏

Option A — Measuring Tape

Wrap a soft tape measure around the widest part of your nail (the base). Write down the measurement in millimeters for each finger.

Most accurate method
📄

Option B — Paper Strip

Cut a thin strip of paper, wrap it snugly around your nail at the widest point, mark where it overlaps, then measure that strip flat with a ruler.

Works great at home
👆

Measure All 10 Nails

Each finger is different! Measure every single nail individually and include all 10 measurements in your order notes for the best fit.

Don't skip the pinky!
💡

Pro Tips

Measure at the base (widest part) of your nail. If you're between two sizes, go one size up — press-ons should never feel tight or pinch the sides.

When in doubt, size up
📸 Tip: You can also trace your nails on a piece of paper, scan or photograph it, and send the image with your order — SetsByRube can match your nails directly from the tracing!

Step 3

Short vs Long — Which Length Is Right?

Not sure how long to go? Here's a quick guide to help you decide.

Short Length

Extends just past the fingertip — super wearable for everyday life. Great for work, school, or if you're new to press-ons. Easy to type, do your hair, anything!

Best for: Everyday wear

Long Length

Dramatic, bold, statement-making. Extends well past the fingertip — perfect for events, photoshoots, birthdays, or any time you want all eyes on your nails ✨

Best for: Special occasions
Step 4

Shape Guide

Not sure which shape suits you? Here's the breakdown of every shape SetsByRube offers.

Almond

Tapers to a soft rounded point. Elegant, feminine, and elongates the fingers. The most flattering on most hand shapes.

Coffin

Tapered sides with a flat top. Bold and dramatic — also called ballerina nails. Super popular for max design sets.

Square

Straight sides with a flat top. Clean, classic, and modern. Works great for French tips and minimal designs.

Round

Straight sides with a softly curved top — follows the natural nail shape. The most low-key and natural-looking option.

Stiletto

Dramatically tapered to a sharp point. The most daring shape — makes a serious statement. Best for long length.
